---
title: See who wants to know when a product is back
tags: restock, back in stock, out of stock, notify me, email, leads, demand
---

When a product is out of stock, customers can leave their email address to hear when it is back. **Restock Leads** lists those requests.

Each row shows the **Product**, its **Reference**, the customer's **Email** and when it was **Requested**.

Use it to see which out-of-stock products people are waiting for, so you can restock the ones in demand first.

The service only collects the requests. It does not send emails, so you contact customers yourself when the product returns.
